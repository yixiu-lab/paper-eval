# Ethics, Data, and Access — Author Statements Required

**Status: MISSING.** No author-approved ethics, data-rights, or model-access statement is included. Complete this checklist based on the actual planned study before submission.

- Identify all data sources, copyright/licensing terms, and redistribution limits.
- Explain whether manuscripts contain confidential, submitted, or otherwise sensitive material and how they are protected.
- State whether human participants or expert raters are involved; describe consent, compensation, privacy, and institutional review requirements where applicable.
- Describe how scores and reports could be misused, including risks to authors, reviewers, or editorial decisions, and the human oversight safeguards.
- Name model and retrieval providers, data handling terms, costs, rate limits, and reasonable access alternatives.
- Explain retention, deletion, access control, and incident handling for uploaded papers and generated reports.
- Disclose known biases, language/domain limitations, and failure modes.

Do not claim ethics approval, consent, or legal permission unless documented by the responsible authors.
