## Call requirements

- Length: approximately 3–5 pages / 1,500–2,000 words, plus references.
- Explain the core intended scientific contribution.
- State what has been done and what remains to be done.
- Identify the key risks to executing the vision.
- Explain how organizational and social science would change if successful.
- Emphasize a novel research process or capability, not merely the use of AI in a conventional study.

## Author inputs needed

- The precise research question and target users in organizational/social science.
- The novel capability beyond the current evidence-grounded paper evaluation prototype.
- Evidence already produced, with links to immutable runs and artifacts.
- Proposed evaluation design, comparison conditions, outcomes, and stopping criteria.
- Human oversight and expert-review plan.
- Main technical, validity, ethical, access, and adoption risks.
- Author list, affiliations, contributions, and any conflicts or commitments.
- References supporting the research gap and contribution.

Do not describe internal self-checks as external validation, or planned work as completed work.
