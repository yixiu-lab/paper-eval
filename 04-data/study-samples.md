# Paper Counts by Study

The main corpus contains **144 papers with completed scores**. The experiment archive contains linked analyses of this evaluation program, including model comparisons, repeated runs, workflow and scoring studies, evidence handling, retrieval, and trace-based diagnostics. The same papers may appear in multiple analyses. Runs, judgments, evidence records, attack cases, and citation candidates are not additional papers.

Study-specific designs and results are documented in the [experiment catalog](../03-experiments/experiment-catalog.md), [historical experiment evidence](../03-experiments/experiment-evidence.md), [robustness experiments](../03-experiments/robustness-experiments.md), and [retrieval evaluation](../03-experiments/retrieval-evaluation.md). Per-paper outcomes for the scored corpus are available in [paper-outcomes.csv](paper-outcomes.csv).
