# Short Proposal Development Brief

## Submission target

Prepare a 1,500–2,000-word Stage 1 proposal (approximately 3–5 pages, plus references) for the *Organization Science* call, “AI-Enabled Frontiers in Organizational Science.”

## Project framing

PaperEval is a prototype for evidence-grounded assessment of management research. It makes rule-level judgments inspectable by linking them to selected paper evidence, recording uncertainty, and applying a declared scoring profile. The proposed research will evaluate this capability against expert judgments and examine its stability, calibration, and scope.

## Proposal structure

1. **Scientific opportunity:** explain why research assessment is a consequential measurement problem for organizational and management science.
2. **Research question:** specify the conditions under which evidence-grounded AI judgments align with expert assessments and remain stable across runs and paper types.
3. **Current foundation:** summarize the 149-PDF V16 audit (146 unique papers, 144 complete reports) and the recorded mechanism, repeatability, and evidence-budget studies.
4. **Study design:** define expert recruitment and annotation, comparison conditions, outcomes, statistical analysis, calibration comparisons, and stopping criteria.
5. **Research capability:** explain how inspectable evidence traces and structured measurement can support new research processes and what the field can learn from the results.
6. **Execution and access:** describe the code/data release plan, source-level access inventory, model alternatives, institutional review pathway, timeline, and team responsibilities.

## Author decisions to resolve

- Confirm the central research question and intended users in organizational/social science.
- Select expert benchmark size, rater qualifications, training, compensation, and adjudication.
- Pre-specify primary outcomes and comparisons, including V16 60/40 and alternative pool ratios.
- Confirm author list, affiliations, contributions, conflicts, and institutional review route.
- Select references supporting the measurement opportunity and research contribution.
- Assign owners and dates for data access, model-provider documentation, and artifact release.

## Call requirements

- Explain the scientific contribution and what has been completed.
- Describe the planned research and its evaluation evidence.
- Identify execution risks with specific mitigation actions.
- Show how organizational and social science could change if the capability succeeds.
- Center a new research process or capability rather than a conventional study that simply uses AI.
